**An SSH project and the server it runs on now share their terminals.** Open a folder over SSH from your laptop and the same folder in a nodeterm running on that server, and both attach the same sessions, so you can switch machines and carry on where you left off. Sessions an older version started on its separate SSH socket are picked up where they are on the first launch after updating: running agents keep going, nothing is restarted and no second copy is opened beside them.

**Claude Remote Control from the terminal header.** Claude terminals have a Remote Control button beside the context meter, and the kanban card modal has one too. Green means the session is reachable from claude.ai or the Claude app. A click runs Claude's own `/remote-control` in that session; the first time, Claude asks its own consent question in the terminal. The button can be hidden in Settings → Appearance.

**Restored output after a reboot is really there.** After a machine reboot, a local terminal's saved output is now written into its new tmux pane, so you can scroll back through it below the "session restored" line. Before, it was drawn and then immediately hidden as tmux took over the screen. SSH-project terminals are not covered yet.

**The ⌘M chat composer grows with your draft.** It grows as you type up to half the panel, and you can drag its top edge to set a height that sticks. A half-typed draft now survives flipping ⌘M off and on, switching projects, reloading and restarting. A line-ending backslash is typed as you wrote it, and an interrupted prompt that wrapped onto several lines is given back to the composer whole.

**Act on several kanban cards at once.** Cmd/Ctrl-click picks cards and Shift-click picks a range; a bar at the bottom moves or deletes the whole selection, and dragging a selected card carries the rest. The board also stops working behind your back: terminals, RUNNING chips and a pinned sidebar underneath it no longer repaint, which removes the stutter with 10 to 20 busy sessions.

**Ctrl+Tab cycles project tabs**, and Ctrl+Shift+Tab goes back, as in a browser. Both can be remapped in Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts.

**A calmer canvas.** Working edges flow for a few seconds and then hold their dashed line, and Reduce Motion now stops every looping animation in the app except progress spinners and the live recording mic.

**Live links are gone.** Sharing one terminal to a browser at `nodeterm.dev/s/…` has been removed, along with its button, menu row and Settings section. The link data it kept on disk is deleted on first launch.
